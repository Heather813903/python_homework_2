Task 5: Ethical Web Scraping

1. Which sections of Wikipedia are restricted from crawling?

Wikipedia restricts crawling of certain sections, including /wiki/Special:, /w/, /api/, and /trap/.

2. Are there specific rules for certain user agents?

Yes. Wikipedia has specific rules for certain bots. For example, MJ12bot is blocked from crawling the entire site, while SemrushBot has a crawl delay of 5 seconds.

3. Why do websites use robots.txt?

Websites use robots.txt to tell web crawlers which parts of their site they can access. Following these rules helps protect websites from too much traffic and promotes responsible web scraping.
